NexJobs Features

AI-powered job search tools that save you hours of manual searching.

Core Features

Everything you need to find the right job, faster.

AI Resume Parsing

Upload your resume in PDF, DOCX, or TXT format. NexJobs uses pdf.js and mammoth.js to extract your skills, experience, education, and projects — all processed in your browser.

Smart Job Matching

Our priority-based scoring engine ranks every job by education match (35%), skills (25%), certifications (15%), projects (15%), and keywords (10%) — so you see the best matches first.

Multi-Platform Search

Search 5+ job APIs simultaneously (Arbeitnow, Remotive, Remote OK, Himalayas, We Work Remotely) and get direct search links for 100+ platforms including LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, and Glassdoor.

Complete Privacy

Everything runs client-side in your browser. Your resume data never leaves your device. No backend servers, no data collection, no tracking cookies.

12 Job Categories

Search across India job portals, software engineering, AI/ML & data science, remote jobs, international portals, internships, freelancing, startups, company career pages, off-campus hiring, government jobs, and quick apply.

Google OAuth Sign-In

Optionally sign in with Google to persist your search history in localStorage. No mandatory accounts — use NexJobs anonymously.
