Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NexJobs — our AI-powered job search platform.

What is NexJobs?

NexJobs is an AI-powered job search platform that lets you upload your resume and instantly search across 100+ job portals. It uses AI to parse your resume, extract your skills and experience, and match you with the best job opportunities — all running in your browser with no backend required.

Is NexJobs free to use?

Yes, NexJobs is completely free to use. There are no premium tiers, subscription fees, or hidden charges. You can upload your resume, search across all 100+ job platforms, and get AI-powered job matches without paying anything.

How does NexJobs parse my resume?

NexJobs uses pdf.js for PDF files and mammoth.js for DOCX files to parse your resume entirely in your browser. It extracts your skills, work experience, education, certifications, and projects. All processing happens locally — your resume data never leaves your device.

Which job platforms does NexJobs search?

NexJobs searches 5+ job APIs directly (Arbeitnow, Remotive, Remote OK, Himalayas, We Work Remotely) and generates pre-filled search links for 100+ platforms including LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, Glassdoor, Monster, Wellfound (AngelList), Internshala, Upwork, Fiverr, GitHub Jobs, Stack Overflow Jobs, and many more across 12 categories.

How does NexJobs match jobs to my resume?

NexJobs uses a priority-based scoring engine that ranks jobs by: Education match (35%), Skills match (25%), Certifications (15%), Projects (15%), and Keywords (10%). Jobs are sorted from highest to lowest match percentage so you see the best opportunities first.

Is my resume data safe and private?

Yes. NexJobs is a fully client-side application — everything runs in your browser. Your resume is never uploaded to any server. All parsing, matching, and search generation happens locally on your device. We collect no personal data and use no tracking cookies.

Do I need to create an account to use NexJobs?

No, you do not need to create an account. NexJobs works completely without sign-up. You can optionally sign in with Google OAuth to persist your search history in localStorage, but it is entirely optional.

What resume formats does NexJobs support?

NexJobs supports three resume formats: PDF, DOCX (Microsoft Word), and TXT (plain text). Simply drag and drop or select your file to get started.

Can NexJobs help me find remote jobs?

Yes! NexJobs has a dedicated Remote Jobs category that searches Remote OK, We Work Remotely, Remotive, Himalayas, and other remote-first job platforms. It also generates search links for remote job listings on LinkedIn, Indeed, and other major platforms.

Can NexJobs help me find government jobs in India?

Yes. NexJobs includes a Government Jobs category that generates search links for UPSC, SSC, Railway, Banking, and PSU job notifications, along with other government job portals in India.

What browsers are supported?

NexJobs works on all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. It requires support for fetch API, AbortSignal.timeout, and DOMParser — all available in modern browsers.

Where is NexJobs hosted?

NexJobs is hosted at nexjobs.in and deployed as a static website. The source code is open and available on GitHub. It can be deployed on any static hosting platform including Vercel, Netlify, GitHub Pages, and Cloudflare Pages.
