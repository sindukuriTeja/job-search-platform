About NexJobs

Making job hunting smarter with AI — built in India, for job seekers everywhere.

Our Mission

NexJobs was built to solve one simple problem: job hunting is too hard. Candidates waste hours applying to the same jobs across dozens of platforms, while employers struggle to find the right talent.

We created NexJobs to change that. By using AI to understand your resume — your skills, experience, education, and projects — we search across 100+ job platforms at once, match you with the best opportunities, and present everything in one place. No backend, no server costs, no sign-up required.

Whether you're a fresher looking for your first role, an experienced professional seeking your next opportunity, or someone exploring remote work — NexJobs helps you find it faster.

What Makes NexJobs Different

We're not another job board. NexJobs is an AI-powered search engine that works across every major platform.

AI Resume Parsing

Upload your resume (PDF, DOCX, or TXT) and our AI extracts your skills, experience, education, and projects — all in the browser, no data sent to servers.

Smart Job Matching

Our priority-based scoring engine ranks jobs by education match (35%), skills (25%), certifications (15%), projects (15%), and keywords (10%).

Multi-Platform Search

Search LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, Glassdoor, Remote OK, Himalayas, and 100+ more platforms with a single query built from your resume.

Privacy First

Everything runs in your browser. Your resume data never leaves your device. No backend servers, no data collection, no tracking.

Zero Setup Required

No account needed. No API keys. No installation. Just open NexJobs, upload your resume, and start searching.

Free Forever

NexJobs is completely free to use. No premium tiers, no hidden charges, no subscription fees. Built as an open tool for job seekers.
