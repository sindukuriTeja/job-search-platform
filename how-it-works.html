How NexJobs Works

Find your dream job in 3 simple steps — no sign-up required.

1

Upload Your Resume

Go to NexJobs.in and drag & drop your resume. We support all major formats:

  • PDF — parsed using pdf.js in your browser
  • DOCX — parsed using mammoth.js in your browser
  • TXT — plain text resumes are read directly

Our AI parser extracts your skills, work experience, education, certifications, and projects — all processed locally in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.

2

Select Job Categories & Location

Choose the categories that match what you're looking for from 12 job categories:

  • 🇮🇳 India Job Portals (Naukri, Indeed India, Instahyre)
  • 💻 Software Engineering (GitHub Jobs, Stack Overflow)
  • 🤖 AI / ML & Data Science
  • 🌐 Remote Jobs (Remote OK, We Work Remotely)
  • ✈️ International Portals (USA, Europe, Global)
  • 🎓 Internships (Internshala, LetsIntern)
  • 👨‍💻 Freelancing (Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer)
  • 🚀 Startup Jobs (Wellfound, AngelList)
  • 🏢 Company Career Pages
  • 🎯 Off-Campus Hiring
  • 🏛️ Government Jobs (UPSC, SSC, Railways, Banking)
  • ⚡ Quick Apply

NexJobs automatically builds optimized search queries from your resume skills and experience level.

3

Search & Get Matched Jobs

NexJobs runs two types of search simultaneously:

  • Live API Search — Fetches real job listings from 5+ job APIs (Arbeitnow, Remotive, Remote OK, Himalayas, We Work Remotely)
  • Direct Search Links — Generates pre-filled search URLs for 100+ platforms (LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, Glassdoor, Monster, and more)

Every job is scored using our priority-based matching engine:

Education 35%
Skills 25%
Certs 15%
Projects 15%
Keywords 10%

Jobs are ranked from highest to lowest match score, so you see the best opportunities first. Click "View & Apply" to go directly to the job posting.
